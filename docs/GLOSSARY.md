# Glossary and acronym registry

The glossary should grow from source-grounded research. Every entry should include expansion, plain-language explanation, context, source, effective date if relevant, and related concepts. Do not silently guess an expansion.

| Term | Plain-language starting definition | Modeling relevance |
|---|---|---|
| CMS | Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, the federal agency administering Medicare and overseeing major federal aspects of Medicaid and CHIP | Federal rules, guidance, data, and oversight |
| Medicaid | Joint federal-state program providing health coverage to eligible groups under applicable federal and state rules | Funding, eligibility, benefits, payment arrangements |
| CHIP | Children's Health Insurance Program | Separate or coordinated coverage pathways for eligible children |
| DHS | Pennsylvania Department of Human Services | State Medicaid administration and program materials |
| OMHSAS | Pennsylvania Office of Mental Health and Substance Abuse Services | Behavioral-health policy and program oversight within DHS |
| BHChoices | Pennsylvania Behavioral HealthChoices program | Behavioral-health managed-care structure for eligible members |
| BH-MCO | Behavioral health managed care organization | Managed behavioral-health services under applicable arrangements |
| CPT | Current Procedural Terminology | Service coding; coding does not itself guarantee coverage or payment |
| ICD | International Classification of Diseases | Diagnosis classification and reporting |
| EPSDT | Early and Periodic Screening, Diagnostic, and Treatment | Medicaid benefit requirements for eligible individuals under age 21 |
| Capitation | Prospective payment for each enrolled member over a defined period, under the terms of an arrangement | Payer/plan revenue and utilization risk |
| Fee-for-service | Payment tied to covered services delivered and billed under applicable rules | Claim-level payment and utilization incentives |
| Value-based payment | Payment arrangement that ties some payment or financial consequences to quality, outcomes, or other performance measures | Incentives, measurement, and risk allocation |
| 1915(b) waiver | A Medicaid waiver authority that can permit certain managed-care arrangements subject to federal approval and conditions | Policy authority; exact waiver terms must be checked |
| PHI | Protected health information under applicable privacy law | Data governance and privacy controls |
| PII | Personally identifiable information; exact scope depends on context and applicable rules | Data minimization and re-identification risk |
