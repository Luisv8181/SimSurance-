## Key content (paraphrased from the page; this is a lookup directory, not a rule)

The page states that under the behavioral-health component of HealthChoices, counties ensure quality care and timely access to mental-health and drug-and-alcohol services, and that each consumer is assigned a Behavioral Health MCO based on county of residence (see verbatim quote below). Members then have a choice of providers within that BH-MCO's network. A chart maps BH-MCOs to the counties they serve; each MCO row carries a link to that MCO's own webpage:

- Community Behavioral Health (CBH) — Philadelphia (1 county)
- Community Care Behavioral Health Organization (CCBHO) — 41 counties: Adams, Allegheny, Berks, Blair, Bradford, Cameron, Carbon, Centre, Chester, Clarion, Clearfield, Clinton, Columbia, Delaware, Elk, Erie, Forest, Greene, Huntingdon, Jefferson, Juniata, Lackawanna, Luzerne, Lycoming, McKean, Mifflin, Montour, Monroe, Northumberland, Pike, Potter, Schuylkill, Snyder, Sullivan, Susquehanna, Tioga, Union, Warren, Wayne, Wyoming, York
- Magellan Behavioral Health of Pennsylvania (MBH) — 7 counties: Bedford, Bucks, Cambria, Lehigh, Montgomery, Northampton, Somerset
- PerformCare — 7 counties: Cumberland, Dauphin, Franklin, Fulton, Lancaster, Lebanon, Perry
- Carelon Health of PA, Inc. — 11 counties: Armstrong, Beaver, Butler, Crawford, Fayette, Indiana, Lawrence, Mercer, Washington, Westmoreland, Venango

Total: 1 + 41 + 7 + 7 + 11 = 67 — Pennsylvania's 67 counties covered exactly once.

## MBH/Magellan label discrepancy — RESOLVED 2026-10-15

The flag first raised 2026-10-12 (recorded in the bhc-providers 10-12 and 10-14 snapshots): the providers page's enrollment map uses the short label "MBH" (Lehigh, Northampton, Bedford, Cambria, Somerset, Bucks, Montgomery) while this directory lists those counties under "Magellan". Re-verification of the directory page today shows the directory's own row reads **"Magellan Behavioral Health of Pennsylvania (MBH)"** — the acronym is defined on the very page that also prints the full name. Same DHS source family, same entity; the providers page map simply abbreviates. No phantom payer. Modeler discipline preserved: this resolves the label, not any claim about networks, benefits, or rates.
