## What the page states (official-site claims only)
- If you receive Medicaid health coverage and need support for mental health
  care or substance use disorder, Behavioral HealthChoices can help.
- Behavioral healthcare covered through Medicaid is overseen by the county's
  mental health office. The county helps determine what services and care a
  person qualifies for and connects them to its behavioral health managed
  care organization (BH-MCO).
- Each county contracts with a BH-MCO for mental health and drug-and-alcohol
  services. The member is assigned a BH-MCO based on county of residence.
- The member has a choice of behavioral health care providers within the
  BH-MCO's network. The MCO sends the member a handbook outlining how to
  access services and the benefits available.
- If enrolled in Medicaid for physical health care, the person also has
  access to mental health and substance use disorder treatment through
  Behavioral HealthChoices.
- If not on Medicaid and having trouble paying for behavioral health care,
  the county mental health office may be able to help; contact them to learn
  about options.
- Medicaid application routes: online through COMPASS
  (www.compass.dhs.pa.gov), in person at the local county assistance office,
  by mail (paper application to the CAO), or by phone via the Consumer
  Service Center (1-866-550-4355).
- The Customer Service Center is available Monday through Friday,
  8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. (Philadelphia number listed separately).
- 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988 for free, confidential
  support 24/7.
- Email contact (OMHSAS) is offered with an explicit warning not to include
  confidential details such as Social Security number or medical
  information.
