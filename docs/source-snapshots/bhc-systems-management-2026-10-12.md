## What the page states (page-level, paraphrased; verbatim quote in snapshot line 6-9)

1. The OMHSAS Division of Systems Management's stated objective is access: "ensure eligible
   individuals can access behavioral health services covered by Medicaid and County
   Base-Funded Mental Health." Its three named jobs: (a) manage interacting state systems
   operations so individuals can access services, (b) manage claim and/or encounter data,
   and (c) conduct business intelligence with the behavioral health data received.
2. The page is explicitly addressed to "OMHSAS Behavioral Health business partners operating
   within HealthChoices and the County Mental Health Base Programs" — NOT a public dataset.
3. Systems Notices table: 37 numbered notices; newest is OMHSAS-2026-001, "OMHSAS
   Sub-Capitation Paymen Supplemental File Reporting Requirements," dated June 9, 2026.
   (Note the page's own typo: "Paymen".)
4. Encounter Documents: companion guides and file layouts for EDI transactions — 837
   encounters (institutional/professional), 834 benefit enrollment/maintenance, unsolicited
   277 status notifications, X12 999 functional acknowledgments, BES report layouts, ESC
   supplemental extract layout (PROMISe error-status encounters), PRV435 provider file,
   PRV720 provider revalidation file, 834 companion guide, edit crosswalks, CCRI edit
   guides and reporting tools.
5. HealthChoices Reports: OMHSAS ran Encounter Data Validation (EDV) studies with each
   primary contractor's BH-MCO, published per-MCO (Beacon Health Options, Community
   Behavioral Health, Community Care Behavioral Health, Magellan, PerformCare), dated 2023;
   plus on-site reviews of claim/encounter data submissions dated 2023 and 2019.
   NOTE: the EDV-study PDFs themselves were NOT read this run (page-level review only).
6. HealthChoices Provider Enrollment Documents: forms for PROMISe Provider Type 07 –
   Capitation.
