## What the page says (page-level verification only — no rules encoded)

First snapshot for this source (previous check 2026-10-10 was page-level, no snapshot
stored). The page sits under the archived 2026 health-rate-filings path and carries two
standing disclaimers: the coverage maps show plan coverage for a past year, are updated
annually, and current plan coverage lives on the ACA Health Rate Filing home page; and
the maps' scope is purchase-only — they do NOT show limits on health care coverage or
the provider network.

Structure: a "What is a Coverage Area?" explainer (each 2026 plan had a unique county
footprint; you must have been a resident of a covered county to enroll; purple counties
= available 2025 and continuing into 2026; light blue = newly available for 2026),
followed by 15 issuer sections, each listing the Pennsylvania counties covered in 2026
with a linked coverage-map image: Ambetter Health of Pennsylvania; Capital Advantage
Assurance Company; Geisinger Health Plan; Geisinger Quality Options; Health Partners
Plans; Highmark, Inc.; Highmark Benefits Group; Highmark Coverage Advantage; Keystone
Health Plan Central; Keystone Health Plan East; Oscar Health Plan of Pennsylvania;
Partners Insurance Company; QCC Insurance Company; UPMC Health Options; UPMC Health
Plan. Footprints range from 2 counties (UPMC Health Plan: Crawford, Clearfield) to
52 counties (UPMC Health Options); Philadelphia-county carriers include Ambetter,
Health Partners Plans, Highmark Benefits Group, Keystone Health Plan East, Oscar,
Partners, and QCC.

## Scope limits

Historical market-availability snapshot for plan year 2026 only: a sales footprint
(where a plan could be purchased), not a provider-network directory and not a rate
record. 2026 boundaries must not be read as the current plan year, and no per-clinician
or per-claim fact can be drawn from a coverage map. `review_required_before_encoding`
stays true — nothing here has been promoted to a rule.
