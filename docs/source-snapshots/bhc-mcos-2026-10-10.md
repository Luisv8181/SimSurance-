## Observed page statements (2026-10-10)

- Under the behavioral health component of HealthChoices, counties ensure quality
  care and timely access to mental health and drug/alcohol services, and coordinate
  with other needed services.
- Each HealthChoices consumer is assigned a Behavioral Health Managed Care
  Organization (BH-MCO) based on county of residence; members choose providers
  within that BH-MCO's network. Each BH-MCO name on the page links to the MCO's
  own website.

## County assignments observed (2026-10-10)

Five BH-MCOs operate statewide, one per county:

- **Community Behavioral Health (CBH):** Philadelphia

## Hard limits — do not exceed

1. This snapshot is a **dated page check only** (2026-10-10). It is not a rule
   encoding and must be re-verified before use in any model.
2. The county mapping assigns the BH-MCO by county of residence; it does **not**
   establish provider credentialing, open network status, benefits, or
   reimbursement rates.
3. A county's BH-MCO is not a fee schedule and does not determine what any MCO
   pays any provider for any service.
4. Contract or rate disputes must be resolved from the PS&R, appendices, or the
   applicable MCO contract — never from this mapping snapshot alone.
5. Do not hard-code this mapping into executable simulation logic without a
   reviewer-approved, versioned source record that cites document version and
   effective dates for the exact modeled period.
