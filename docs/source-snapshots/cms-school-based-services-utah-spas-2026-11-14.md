## Resolution of the Utah link-target mismatch
- The index line reads "Utah SPA 21-0009. (approved August 15, 2022)"
  and its link target is UT-21-0019.pdf. The line conflates TWO
  different SPAs:
  - The NUMBER 21-0009 belongs to the rehabilitative mental-health SPA,
    approved May 26, 2022, effective July 1, 2021. It has nothing to do
    with school-based services.
  - The DATE August 15, 2022 belongs to SPA 21-0019, the school-based
    payments SPA — which is exactly the file the link serves.
- Verdict: the label is wrong, the target is right, and the date is
  right for the target. A reader who follows the link lands on the
  correct document; a reader who cites the label quotes the wrong SPA
  number. Cite the document (21-0019), never the link text alone.
- The index's grouping is CORRECT for the actual document: 21-0019 is a
  cost methodology with IDEA services — a cost-based payment model, and
  school-setting services require a valid IEP. The grouping was right
  even while the number was wrong.
- The 2026-11-10 "(approved October 14, 2021)" Utah flicker appears in
  NEITHER PDF and remains a single unreproduced event — flagged, not
  promoted.
- Tally closed: mismatch present on the last four live loads, 4 present /

  in the label, right file in the target), so no further tally is needed.
