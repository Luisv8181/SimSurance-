## What the page states (official-site claims only)
- CMS notes it has "issued school-based services (SBS) guidance, most notably the
  2023 Comprehensive Guide to Medicaid Services and Administrative Claiming."
- CMS has provided sample State Plan Amendments (SPAs), Time Study
  Implementation Plans (TSIPs), Administrative Claiming Plans, and Cost Reports.
- States can reach the School-Based Services Technical Assistance Center (TAC)
  at SchoolBasedServices@cms.hhs.gov.
- TAC resources listed: Medicaid SBS 101 Quick Guide (for SMAs, SEAs, LEAs —
  overview of terms, guidance, state flexibilities); Readiness Checklist Tool
  (for SMAs drafting an SBS SPA or adopting flexibilities from the 2023
  Informational Bulletin and Comprehensive Guide); Understanding Cost Pools job
  aid (for LEAs — what cost pools are, examples, how an LEA uses them); How to
  Answer RMTS job aid (LEA guide to Random Moment Time Study requirements and
  best practices); Federal Documentation Requirements for LEAs (CMS and ED
  documentation requirements for service claims, cost reporting, time studies,
  audit compliance); specialized-transportation reimbursement resource;
  Considerations for Developing a TSIP (SPA elements, summer-period treatment,
  participant lists, training).
- CMS guidance listed: 2023 "Delivering Services in School-Based Settings: A
  Comprehensive Guide to Medicaid Services and Administrative Claiming"; May
  2023 Informational Bulletin on coverage, billing, reimbursement, documentation
  and administrative claiming flexibilities; August 2022 Informational Bulletin
  on funding, documentation and expanding services; 2014 SMD Letter #14-006 on
  Medicaid payment for services provided without charge ("free care");
  2022 HHS school-based health services resources.
- State SBS examples: SPAs using cost methodology with services expanded beyond
  IDEA to any Medicaid-enrolled child (AZ, CO, IL, NM with approval dates);
  cost-methodology IDEA-services SPAs (UT); cost-based-rate methodology with
  expanded services (OR); SPAs paying regular Medicaid fee-schedule rates (NV
  expanded-services, WY IDEA-only); a Utah time-study implementation guide; and
  cost reports (CA DHCS LEA Medi-Cal Billing Option Program, CO, NH).
- The page carries a disclaimer that links to non-U.S.-government sites do not
  constitute endorsement by CMS/HHS.
