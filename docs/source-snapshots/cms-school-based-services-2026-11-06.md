  preserving the error's history. The tally (four reads for July 18, 2022
  against one snapshot for January 6, 2022) is now moot: the document,

  the conflict was flagged, neither date was quoted or modeled while it

## Document-level facts from the Wyoming SPA package (read, not modeled)
- The amendment "proposes to add School-Based Services, which allows the
  State Medicaid Agency to reimburse local education agencies (LEAs) for
  providing health related services to Medicaid eligible students in a
  school-based setting" (approval letter, verbatim).
- Reimbursement methodology (Attachment 4.19B, pages 1-2): School-Based
  Services are reimbursed "according to the fixed fee schedule"; "state-
  developed fee schedule rates are the same for both governmental and
  private providers of SBS"; the rate "was set as of January 1, 2021 and
  is effective for services provided on or after that date"; rates are
  published on the Wyoming Department of Health's website
  (https://www.wyomingmedicaid.com/portal/fee-schedules).
- Covered services listed: (1) physical therapy, (2) occupational
  therapy, (3) speech therapy, language disorders and audiology services,
  (4) nursing services including registered nurses, licensed practical
  nurses and licensed advanced practice registered nurse practitioners,
  (5) psychological services. "All providers and services are paid the
  same as providers and services outside of the school based setting."
- The approved SPA pages show the approval-date field blank and the
  effective date as July 1, 2022; the letter supplies the approval date.
- This is one state's fee-schedule design for IDEA-only services. It is
  not a rate, a coverage rule for any other state, or evidence about any
  other SPA on the index page. The other seven state SPA documents were
  NOT opened this run.
