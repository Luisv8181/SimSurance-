## What the page states (official-site claims only)
- CMS notes it has "issued school-based services (SBS) guidance, most notably the
  2023 Comprehensive Guide to Medicaid Services and Administrative Claiming."
- CMS has provided sample State Plan Amendments (SPAs), Time Study
  Implementation Plans (TSIPs), Administrative Claiming Plans, and Cost Reports.
- States can reach the School-Based Services Technical Assistance Center (TAC)
  at SchoolBasedServices@cms.hhs.gov.
- TAC resources listed: Medicaid SBS 101 Quick Guide (for SMAs, SEAs, LEAs);
  Readiness Checklist Tool (for SMAs drafting an SBS SPA or adopting 2023
  flexibilities); Understanding Cost Pools job aid (for LEAs); How to Answer
  RMTS job aid (LEA guide to Random Moment Time Study); Federal Documentation
  Requirements for LEAs (CMS and ED requirements, audit compliance);
  specialized-transportation reimbursement resource; Considerations for
  Developing a TSIP (SPA elements, summer-period treatment, participant lists,
  training).
- CMS guidance listed: 2023 Comprehensive Guide; May 2023 Informational Bulletin;
  August 2022 Informational Bulletin; 2014 SMD Letter #14-006 (free care);
  2022 HHS resources; NPPES NPI application link.
- State SBS examples listed (grouped by payment design):
  - Cost methodology, expanded services beyond IDEA to any Medicaid enrolled
    child: AZ SPA 21-0006 (approved 2021-10-25), CO SPA 19-0021 (approved
    2020-02-11), IL SPA 21-0008 (approved 2023-04-18), NM SPA 22-0004 (approved
    2023-05-18).
  - Cost methodology, IDEA services only: UT SPA 21-0009 (approved 2022-08-15).
  - Cost-based rate methodology, expanded services for any Medicaid-enrolled
    child: OR SPA 19-0011 (approved 2023-05-18).
  - Fee-schedule-rate SPAs: NV SPA 19-0005 (expanded services, approved
    2019-10-24); WY SPA 22-0001 (IDEA only, approved 2022-01-06).
- Time Study Implementation Plan example: Utah School-Based Medicaid Time
  Study Implementation Guide. Cost-report examples: California DHCS LEA
  Medi-Cal Billing Option Program; Colorado DOE School Health Services
  Program; New Hampshire Medicaid to Schools.
- Disclaimer present: third-party links are "for reference only; linking to a
  non-United States Government website does not constitute an endorsement by
  CMS, HHS, or any of their employees."
