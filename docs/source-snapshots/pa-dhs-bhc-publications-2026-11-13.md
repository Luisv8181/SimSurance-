## What the page does NOT establish

- The children's-services table is a funding-availability map, not a covered-benefit
  schedule and not a rate document. An X is a funding mark, never a promise that
  a child can get an appointment — the page itself says availability varies and
  directs Medicaid enrollees to check with the BH-MCO.
- No coverage rule, payment rate, credentialing standard, or contract term. Those
  live inside the linked documents, which must be opened and read for
  version/effective date before any claim is encoded.
- "Last updated: October 2026" is the only dateable fact on this index page; the
  page carries no revision history, so "current" is unknowable and claims carry
  the check date.
