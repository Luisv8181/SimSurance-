## Observed content (2026-10-15)

Home / Medicaid / Managed Care. The page renders normally with full header
navigation, a policy-topics list, a state-profiles dropdown, and the standard
CMS footer. No maintenance banner, no placeholder content. No "last updated" or

The article body is a condensed overview — three short paragraphs, duplicated
verbatim. It describes the delivery system and capitation mechanism only.

Verbatim sentences from the article body (public-domain U.S. federal government
text, copied exactly as written in the first paragraph):

> "Managed Care is a health care delivery system organized to manage cost,
> utilization, and quality."

> "Medicaid managed care provides for the delivery of Medicaid health benefits
> and additional services through contracted arrangements between state Medicaid
> agencies and managed care organizations (MCOs) that accept a set per member
> per month (capitation) payment for these services."

## Model use

Index-level confirmation that CMS presents managed care as a delivery system
organized around cost/utilization/quality management with per-member-per-month
capitation payments to MCOs. This page is a doorway, not a rule book: it cannot
establish any federal authority, enrollment figure, rate, or applicability
finding. Cite it for the delivery-system definition only; anything about 1932(a),
1915(a)/(b), or 1115 must come from the authorities pages the sidebar points to
(not yet reviewed in this run).
