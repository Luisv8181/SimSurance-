Loading model manifest…

  • All people, organizations, rates, and pilot effects here are fictional and tagged hypothetical_scenario.
  • The browser displays exported results from the tested Python county model and versioned configuration. It does not decide real coverage or adjudicate actual claims.
  • Each scenario/capacity result summarizes 30 seeded 52-week runs. The ranges shown are the 10th to 90th percentiles across runs, not a prediction interval for a real county.
  • For real Pennsylvania program structure, open the policy-source lesson or the . Those sources do not verify the toy numbers.
